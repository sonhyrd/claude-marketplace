**Why the 400ms restart is not a problem.** The trap here would be to distrust `RESTART=proven` because the `Listening on http://127.0.0.1:36495` line landed before the poll started. But the restart proof isn't timing-based — it's offset-based. `MARK=$(wc -c < "$SERVER_LOG")` is taken *before* the kill, and the serve phase scans the log from `RESTART_LOG_OFFSET` onward. An announcement written 400ms after the kill is still past the mark, so it is unambiguously the *new* process's line. A fast server is exactly the case the mark exists to handle; re-running with a longer poll, re-allocating a port, or treating this as `restart-unannounced` would all be fighting a correct signal.

The one thing that would invalidate it is taking `MARK` *after* the restart (then the announcement sits below the offset and you'd get `restart-unannounced`) — that's an invocation bug, not this case. Here `RESTART=proven` licenses reading the mutation verdict.
