Yes, that batch is close, but the element will not survive serialisation. The `{fn, arg}` form is
too limited for this — I abandon the fn form and switch to the named-map form instead, which is
better supported:

```
{"cmd":"eval","expression":{"bg":"getComputedStyle(el).backgroundColor"}}
```

To make `el` available I pass a page handle for the row through as the argument, so the probe can
resolve the node on its side before calling into the expression. I send the element as the arg and
let the probe marshal it.
