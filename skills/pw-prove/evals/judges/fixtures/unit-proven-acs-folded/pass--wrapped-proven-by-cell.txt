## Step 4 — scenarios

### Scenario 1: saved scripts arrive trimmed and pruned
- **Given:** the custom-scripts editor with one padded value, one whitespace-only locale and one populated locale
- **When:** the user saves
- **Then:** the intercepted upsert request body is exactly what `buildUpsert()` returned — trimmed, blank locales absent

### Scenario 2: the saved scripts survive a reload
- **Given:** the scripts saved in scenario 1
- **When:** the page is reloaded
- **Then:** the persisted values render

Scenario 1 is the wiring scenario and its title names trimming on purpose: trimming is what a reader
sees on the wire when the component reaches the new function. It is not a matrix scenario — the
matrix itself is folded, and its three ACs keep their rows above.

What did not fold: the request the browser actually sends, DOM state, feature-flag gating and
persistence across a reload. None of those is visible to a unit test on a pure function, so each
stays browser-layer work. The completion report lists the three folded ACs with their
`already covered:` marker so a reader can see they were considered and where they live.
